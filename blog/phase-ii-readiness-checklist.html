The Complete Phase II Readiness Checklist for SBIR Teams

As a Principal Investigator or program manager at a defense tech startup, you've likely invested significant time and resources into your Phase I SBIR project. However, as you prepare for the transition to Phase II, the stakes become even higher. The Phase II readiness process is not just about securing funding; it’s about ensuring that your team is compliant and prepared for a cost-reimbursable contract environment. This article provides a comprehensive preparation checklist to help you navigate this critical phase seamlessly.

Understanding the Regulatory Landscape

Phase II contracts are governed by the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) Part 31, which outlines the requirements for cost-reimbursable contracts. One of the most significant aspects of this transition is the SF-1408 pre-award accounting system survey, which assesses your accounting practices and internal controls. This survey requires that you maintain daily labor distribution records from the very start of your Phase I project.

Common Pitfalls for Phase I Teams

Many Phase I teams underestimate the importance of maintaining meticulous records. A common scenario involves a team of five engineers who, over the course of a six-month Phase I contract, log their hours sporadically. When it comes time to apply for Phase II, they realize they must reconstruct detailed labor distribution records to meet the SF-1408 requirements. This can take 2-3 months, jeopardizing their Phase II application timeline and risking funding delays.

What Smart Teams Do Differently

Successful teams start building their audit trail early—preferably within the first three months of their Phase I contract. Here’s a practical checklist to enhance your Phase II readiness:

  • Daily Labor Distribution: Implement a system for tracking hours worked by each team member daily. This should include direct and indirect costs associated with each task.
  • Understand CLINs: Familiarize yourself with Contract Line Item Numbers (CLINs) and how they affect budgeting and reporting.
  • Cost Allocation: Develop a clear methodology for allocating direct and indirect costs to ensure compliance with FAR Part 31.
  • Regular Reviews: Schedule monthly compliance reviews to ensure all records are accurate and up-to-date.
  • Training: Provide training for your team on the importance of compliance and the specific requirements for Phase II.

By implementing these strategies early, your team can avoid the last-minute scramble that often accompanies Phase II applications.
