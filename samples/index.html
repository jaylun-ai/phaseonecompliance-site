Every figure is synthetic

Vantage Aerospace Systems is fictional. So is contract N68335-26-C-0147 and every engineer, hour, and CLIN value in the package. Every PDF carries a diagonal “SAMPLE OUTPUT — SYNTHETIC DATA” watermark that can't be cropped out without removing content. Real client data in a marketing asset is a confidentiality breach — particularly under DFARS 252.204-7012.

The methodology is real

CLIN-to-SOW mapping, FAR Part 31.205 allowability rules, FAR 31.201-4 allocability tests, DCAA CAM §5-909 contemporaneous evidence standard, SF-1408 pre-award accounting-system criteria — these are the rules PhaseOne's production pipeline applies to real clients. The fiction is the data, not the logic.
